The Pit

thepit.cloud ↗

Evaluation platform for multi-agent systems, with observable traces, rubric scoring, failure tagging, and cost tracking per run. Agent prompts are hashed onto Ethereum L2 for independent attestation. It answers the commercial questions behind AI tooling: what did this run cost, what did it catch, and is the extra model call worth it.
