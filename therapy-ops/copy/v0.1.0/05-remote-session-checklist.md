# Remote-session checklist

Status: Draft for review

Version: v0.1.0

## Before the first session

- Use a laptop or desktop computer with a webcam where possible.
- Check that Google Meet opens in a supported browser.
- Find a private place where you are unlikely to be overheard or interrupted.
- Keep a telephone available in case the connection fails.
- Confirm the telephone number, GP details, and emergency contact supplied at
  intake remain accurate.
- Open the client-visible collaboration workspace.
- Tell the practitioner about accessibility needs or technical barriers.

## At the beginning of each session

The practitioner may confirm:

- the client's current physical location;
- whether the client can speak privately;
- the fallback telephone number;
- whether anyone else is present;
- whether transcription or AI-processing choices have changed;
- whether the client can access the shared workspace.

## If the connection fails

1. Try to rejoin the same Google Meet.
2. The practitioner may call the agreed fallback number.
3. Decide together whether to continue by telephone or rearrange.
4. If there is an immediate safety concern and contact cannot be restored, the
   practitioner may use the agreed location, GP, or emergency-contact
   information in line with the therapy agreement.

## Shared workspace

The shared document or slide deck is for agreed collaborative material. It is
not a place for urgent messages and it is not the full clinical record.

Do not add information about another identifiable person unless it is necessary
for the therapeutic work.

## Emergencies

The therapy service does not provide continuous monitoring or an out-of-hours
crisis response. Emergency and urgent-care instructions will be confirmed
before operational use.
