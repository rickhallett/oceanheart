## 1. Purpose and scope

We are agreeing to remote individual therapy for an adult client located in the
United Kingdom. Therapy involves uncertainty. I will work carefully and
collaboratively, but I cannot guarantee a particular result.

This service is not suitable for emergency or crisis response. It is not a
replacement for urgent medical care, emergency services, or continuous risk
monitoring.

## 2. Sessions

Sessions are normally {{sessionLengthMinutes}} minutes and are held through
Google Meet. We will agree session dates together.

The client agrees, where reasonably possible, to join:

- from a private and safe place;
- using a laptop or desktop computer with a webcam;
- with access to the shared collaboration workspace;
- from a location that can be identified if urgent help is required.

If technology fails, I may contact the client using the agreed telephone number.
We will decide whether to continue by telephone, rearrange, or end the session.

## 5. Confidentiality

I will treat the work as confidential and will explain who may have access to
the records. Total confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Information may need to be discussed or disclosed:

- in professional supervision, using the minimum identifying information
  reasonably necessary;
- when I believe there is a serious risk of harm to the client or another
  person;
- for safeguarding purposes;
- when disclosure is required by law or a court;
- when another professional or emergency service needs information to protect
  health or safety;
- when the client asks me to communicate with another person or organisation.

Where it is safe and lawful to do so, I will try to discuss a disclosure with
the client first.

## 6. Records, transcription, and AI processing

The privacy notice explains the records I keep, the purposes for which they are
used, who can access them, and how long they are retained.

Google Meet transcription and AI-supported processing are governed by a
separate schedule. Audio or video recording is outside this MVP and requires a
separate future agreement.

AI-generated material is treated as a draft. I remain responsible for checking
clinical notes, formulations, treatment plans, evaluations, reports, letters,
and other professional output.

## 7. Collaborative workspace

We may maintain a client-visible Google document or slide deck. It is intended
to support shared understanding and provide useful material for the client to
keep.

The shared workspace is not the complete clinical record. Private notes,
transcripts, supervision material, risk records, and provisional professional
thinking will not be shared into it automatically.

Either person may propose ending therapy. Where possible, we will use a session
to review the work, consider next steps, and close the shared materials.
