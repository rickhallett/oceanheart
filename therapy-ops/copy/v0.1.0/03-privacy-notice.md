## Information collected

Depending on what is necessary for the service, records may include:

- name, date of birth, address, email address, and telephone number;
- GP and emergency-contact details;
- appointment, invoice, payment-status, and communication records;
- intake information and relevant health information;
- clinical notes, risk and safeguarding records, goals, formulations, plans,
  outcome reviews, reports, and letters;
- Google Meet transcripts where separately agreed;
- material created in the client-visible collaboration workspace;
- records showing which agreements and consent settings applied.

Payment-card details are not collected or stored.

## Purposes

Information may be used to:

- assess whether the service is suitable;
- arrange, provide, review, and close therapy;
- manage risk, safeguarding, and continuity of care;
- communicate with the client and, when authorised or otherwise justified,
  relevant professionals or emergency contacts;
- manage appointments, invoices, payments, complaints, and insurance matters;
- prepare and review provisional formulations, treatment plans, progress
  evaluations, reports, and letters;
- meet professional, legal, insurance, and record-keeping responsibilities.

Information will not be used for advertising, public case studies, or training
general-purpose AI models without a new and specific agreement.

## Access and sharing

Access is limited to people who need the information for an agreed purpose.
This may include:

- the practitioner;
- the practitioner's professional supervisor;
- approved technology processors;
- another professional, emergency contact, safeguarding body, insurer, legal
  adviser, court, or emergency service where the client has authorised sharing
  or another lawful justification applies.

Administrative, clinical, and client-visible records are kept separate.
Contact details should be kept separately from private session notes where
practicable.

## Accuracy and AI-supported drafts

Automated transcripts and AI-generated drafts can be incomplete or wrong. The
practitioner reviews material before relying on it professionally. Clients are
encouraged to raise misunderstandings or factual errors.

No solely automated system decides diagnosis, treatment, risk, disclosure,
eligibility, or termination of therapy.

## Client rights

Subject to applicable law, clients may ask for:

- access to their personal information;
- correction of inaccurate information;
- restriction or objection to some processing;
- deletion where there is no overriding reason to retain information;
- transfer of information in an appropriate format;
- information about how a decision or record was produced.

Withdrawing an optional consent affects future processing. It does not
automatically require deletion of information that must be retained for another
lawful and explained reason.

Questions or requests should be sent to {{contactEmail}}.

Clients may also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. The
practice complaints route is {{complaintsRoute}}.

## Changes
