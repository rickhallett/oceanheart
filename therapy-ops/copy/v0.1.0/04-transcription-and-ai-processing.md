# Transcription and AI-processing schedule

Status: Draft for review

Version: v0.1.0

This schedule is separate from the therapy agreement so that capture and
AI-processing choices are clear and reviewable.

## 1. What is proposed

The proposed service may use:

- Google Meet transcription to create a written transcript of spoken words;
- approved private AI tools to help the practitioner draft and review
  professional material;
- a client-visible Google document or slide deck for collaborative work.

Google Meet transcription is not the same as audio or video recording. Audio
and video recording are outside this MVP.

## 2. Why the tools may be used

Subject to the choices below, transcripts and clinical records may be used to
support:

- provisional formulation;
- collaborative goal setting and treatment planning;
- progress and outcome review;
- preparation and checking of clinical notes;
- preparation of a client-visible summary or diagram;
- drafting reports and letters requested as part of the work;
- identifying inconsistencies, missing information, or questions for the
  practitioner to review.

The tools are intended to support the practitioner's attention and follow-up.
They do not guarantee faster treatment or better outcomes.

## 3. Safeguards

- The practitioner remains responsible for all clinical decisions.
- AI output is a draft until reviewed by the practitioner.
- No AI tool may make a solely automated decision about diagnosis, treatment,
  risk, confidentiality, disclosure, or ending therapy.
- Only approved processors and products listed in the privacy notice may be
  used.
- Material must not be used to train a general-purpose model unless a new,
  specific agreement is made.
- Client-identifying information should be minimised when the approved purpose
  does not require it.
- Internal records are not added to the client-visible workspace
  automatically.

## 4. Per-session control

The practitioner will not configure transcription to start automatically.

At the beginning of a session the practitioner will check whether the agreed
capture setting still applies. Google Meet also shows participants when
transcription is active.

Transcription can be stopped and restarted. If either person asks to stop, the
practitioner will stop it as soon as reasonably possible. An off-record section
will not be copied into later notes from memory as if it had been transcribed.

## 7. Withdrawal and changes

The client can ask to change an optional choice for future sessions or future
processing. The practitioner will record the new version and effective date.

another lawful and explained basis. The practitioner will explain what can be
deleted, what must be retained, and why.
