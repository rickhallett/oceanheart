# How we will work together

Status: Draft for review

Version: v0.1.0

This short guide explains the practical shape of the service. The therapy
agreement, privacy notice, and transcription and AI-processing schedule provide
the full terms.

## The service

Sessions are held remotely through Google Meet and normally last
{{sessionLengthMinutes}} minutes. We begin with a block of
{{sessionsPerBlock}} sessions, paid in advance. The block gives us enough time
to establish a shared understanding, agree useful goals, and review whether the
work is helping.

Therapy is collaborative. Alongside our conversations, we may use a shared
Google document or slide deck as a working map. It can hold agreed goals,
diagrams, exercises, explanations, and other material that you may want to keep.
This client-visible workspace is separate from my private clinical record.

## Technology

Please join from a private place using a laptop or desktop computer with a
webcam where possible. A larger screen makes it easier to work together on the
shared workspace. If this creates an accessibility or practical difficulty,
please tell me so we can agree an alternative.

At the start of remote sessions I may confirm:

- your current location;
- that you can speak privately;
- the best telephone number to use if the connection fails;
- whether the agreed transcription or AI-processing settings still apply.

## Transcription and AI-supported work

Google Meet transcription and approved private AI tools may be used only under
the separate transcription and AI-processing schedule. These tools support my
work. They do not replace my clinical judgement, and they do not make
independent decisions about diagnosis, treatment, risk, or confidentiality.

## Payment and cancellations

The fee is {{sessionFee}} per session. A block contains
{{sessionsPerBlock}} sessions and is paid {{paymentTiming}}.

Please give at least {{cancellationNoticeHours}} hours' notice if you need to
cancel or move a session. The therapy agreement explains charges, exceptions,
refunds, and what happens when I cancel.

## Emergencies

This is not an emergency or crisis service. I do not provide continuous
monitoring or an out-of-hours response. The therapy agreement explains the
contact and risk arrangements that apply.

## Questions

Please ask about anything that feels unclear, uncomfortable, or overly formal.
The purpose of these documents is to make our work understandable and
predictable, not to prevent discussion.
