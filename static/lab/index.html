Four ways this site could stop behaving.

Experimental redesigns built on Canvas UI, a WebGL and html-in-canvas component library. Deliberately incoherent as a set: each one takes a single strand of what this site is about and turns it all the way up. None of this is the real homepage. That is the point.

01 / VARIANT

Undertow

The site is named after an ocean and has never once looked like one. Ink and oxblood disperse through pointer-driven water over the same warm paper.

liquid · ripple 02 / VARIANT

Decrypt

The homepage as a verification terminal. Copy resolves from scrambled glyphs into verified text; the work presents as audit traces.

glyph-rain · hand-rolled scramble-reveal 03 / VARIANT

Clarity

Fifteen years of making opaque things legible, played as optics. Frost clears where you look; a lens invites close reading of the work.

frost · glass · magnify 04 / VARIANT

Sometimes Completely Wrong

Confident, coherent, and periodically corrupted. The page glitches mid-sentence, the verification layer catches it, and the text restores clean. The failure loop is the pitch.

glitch · vhs · shatter

Built with canvasui.dev vanilla components inlined per page. Full effects need Chrome's html-in-canvas origin trial; every page is designed so the WebGL-overlay fallback is the primary experience. Local branch claude/canvas-ui-lab, not deployed.
