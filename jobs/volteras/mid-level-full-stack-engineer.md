# Volteras - Mid-Level Full-Stack Engineer

Captured: 2026-06-29T11:29:04+01:00

## Role snapshot

Volteras is hiring a London-based mid-level full-stack engineer to work across its connected vehicle data platform. The role sits close to product and engineering, with end-to-end ownership across frontend, backend, API design, integrations, and customer-facing functionality.

The advertised compensation band on Welcome to the Jungle is GBP 45,000 to GBP 65,000, with stock options and benefits. Work pattern is listed as fully remote.

## Company context from the job

Volteras describes itself as a company unlocking EV ecosystem data so fleets, insurers, energy companies, finance providers, and other customers can build products on top of connected vehicle information. The platform emphasizes direct OEM access rather than aftermarket hardware or scraping.

The public company positioning is:

- one API for connected vehicle data,
- support for more than 35 automakers,
- vehicle eligibility and consent flows,
- fleet visibility and dashboards,
- battery health and valuation signals,
- smart charging and energy integrations,
- usage-based pricing.

## Core responsibilities

The job expects the engineer to:

- build and maintain full-stack product features;
- work with frontend and backend code, especially TypeScript and modern web frameworks;
- design and integrate APIs;
- contribute to data-heavy product workflows around vehicles, fleets, charging, and battery health;
- collaborate closely with product and engineering leadership;
- ship features that are robust enough for customer-facing connected-vehicle workflows.

## Required / expected skills

The role appears strongest for someone with:

- production TypeScript;
- modern frontend experience, especially React-style product UI work;
- backend/API experience in Node or equivalent web-service stacks;
- database and data modelling comfort;
- pragmatic product engineering habits;
- ability to own ambiguous slices without heavy supervision.

## Desirable signals

The secondary role page adds useful signals that may come up in screening:

- experience with streaming data, event-driven systems, or message queues;
- experience with API-heavy integrations;
- comfort with AI-assisted engineering tools, including CLI-based coding agents;
- interest in climate, energy, transport, fleets, or EV infrastructure;
- ability to work with remote, cross-functional teams.

## Benefits and process

Listed benefits include remote-first work, flexibility, meaningful stock options, pension, private medical insurance, laptop/equipment, learning support, and team socials.

The application process is listed as:

1. Talent introduction.
2. Technical interview.
3. Take-home or practical exercise.
4. Final conversation with senior stakeholders.

## Read for Richard

This is not an AI-reliability role in the Normal Computing sense. It is a full-stack product/integration role where the AI angle should be used selectively:

- as evidence of modern tooling fluency;
- as evidence of building reliable systems around uncertain data and behaviour;
- as evidence of traceability, validation, and product judgement.

The call should centre on production full-stack delivery, API/data workflows, customer-facing product ownership, and fast domain ramping. The CV's recent AI work is a differentiator, but the strongest fit is still TypeScript, Python/Node, React/Vue/Next, product shipping, integration work, and comfort turning messy workflows into usable tools.
