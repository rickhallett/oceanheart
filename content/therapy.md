I occasionally offer private therapy when the work and practical circumstances
are a good fit. The initial service is for individual adults who will normally
be located in the United Kingdom during sessions.

## How the work is structured

Sessions take place through Google Meet. We normally begin with a block of four
sessions, paid in advance, so that there is enough continuity to establish a
shared understanding, agree useful goals, and review whether the work is
helping.

The service is collaborative. We may use a shared Google document or slide deck
as a working map for goals, diagrams, explanations, exercises, and other
material worth keeping. This is separate from my private clinical record.

## Technology-supported practice

Where specifically agreed, Google Meet transcription and approved private AI
tools may help me prepare and review provisional formulations, treatment plans,
progress reviews, client-visible summaries, reports, and letters.

These tools support my work. They do not make clinical decisions, and they are
not used without clear information about the purpose and the applicable client
choice. I remain responsible for reviewing professional output.

## Practical requirements

A laptop or desktop computer with a webcam is strongly preferred. A larger
screen makes it easier to use the shared working space. You will also need a
private place where you are unlikely to be overheard.

Accessibility and technology needs can be discussed before therapy begins.

## Privacy and recording

An initial enquiry should contain as little sensitive information as possible.
If we decide to continue, you will receive a private intake form, a plain
language therapy agreement, a privacy notice, and a separate transcription and
AI-processing schedule.

Google Meet transcription is not the same as audio or video recording. Audio
and video recording are not part of the current service design.

## Cancellations

The proposed cancellation period is 48 hours. The complete agreement will
explain payment, rearrangement, reasonable exceptions, refunds, and what
happens if I cancel.

## Important limitation

This is not an emergency or crisis service and is not monitored continuously.
Do not use an email enquiry to request urgent help.
