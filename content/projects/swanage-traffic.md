## What it is

A paid client build for a local traffic campaign: a public-facing campaign site with a supporter registration platform and a built-in admin workflow for managing submissions. Built for real public use, with clear content flows, form handling, and maintainable components.

## What I built

- **Registration platform**, public sign-up with validated form handling.
- **Admin workflow**, an internal surface for reviewing and managing submissions.
- **Content + components**, maintainable, with clear flows for a non-technical campaign team.
- **Live deployment**, shipped and serving the public.

## Stack

Astro, React, Vercel. Live at [swanagetraffic.org.uk](https://www.swanagetraffic.org.uk).
