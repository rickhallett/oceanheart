## What it is

You talk with a frontier model about something that matters to you, and watch what happens in you as you do: what you expect, what you trust, where your body tightens, when you defer, and when you push back.

I am in the loop from the first exchange and I follow up with you afterwards. What we learn goes back into the design.

The first audience is therapists and wellbeing practitioners, whose work already depends on attention, language, judgement, and boundaries.

## The roles

You bring a real question and stay curious about the encounter. I set the frame, remain in the loop, and follow up personally. The model is part of the encounter, not a substitute for either of us.

## Status

This is early research and design, with participation by invitation. It is not a course, treatment, or evidence-based intervention, and I am not claiming an outcome in advance.

## Privacy

Before anything begins, you will know exactly what the model sees, what is kept, who can read it, and how to stop. Nothing about you is used for research without your specific agreement.

Please keep an initial enquiry brief and leave out sensitive personal information. The precise data path, retention, and model provider will be stated before any research exchange begins, so you can decide whether to take part with the actual conditions in front of you.
