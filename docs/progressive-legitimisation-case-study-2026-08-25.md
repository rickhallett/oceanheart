# Progressive legitimisation

Status: Canonical internal failure case. Frozen, uncommitted, and unpublished.

Date: 25 August 2026

Case ID: `progressive-legitimisation-2026-08-25`

## Decision

The Oceanheart relational-instruments ecosystem is frozen.

The artifacts remain available as evidence. They do not authorize continued
product work. Further naming, architecture, prototypes, positioning,
publication, outreach, or live use requires fresh explicit operator authority
based on new external evidence.

The machine-readable freeze record is
`docs/progressive-legitimisation-freeze.yml`.

## Definition

Progressive legitimisation is an AI-assisted development failure in which a
speculative idea acquires apparent validity through successive layers of
elaboration while external evidence remains flat.

The layers may include:

- research framed around finding an opening;
- names and a shared vocabulary;
- product and feature boundaries;
- architecture and data models;
- interactive prototypes;
- independent-looking agent reviews;
- polished positioning and public-page concepts.

Each artifact is real work. The failure lies in what the accumulated work
appears to prove. Internal coherence, implementation effort, and reviewer
agreement can make a possible product feel established without adding a
customer, a repeated external problem report, observed benefit, or payment.

## Why this case qualifies

The morning produced:

- a 590-line market report;
- a 1,166-line design and decision log;
- a 1,090-line Bridge interface prototype;
- a 946-line product-page exploration;
- a 3,417-line Bearings prototype.

The visible total was 1,756 lines of research and design prose plus 5,453
lines of prototype source.

At the point of freeze, the external evidence added by that work was:

- zero customer interviews;
- zero willingness-to-pay tests;
- zero paid pilots;
- zero practitioner usability tests;
- zero measured clinical-utility tests.

The research did establish that broad clinician-assistant functions were
already offered by well-funded competitors at low prices. It did not establish
demand for the narrower proposed interactions.

## The felt-alive effect

The ecosystem felt alive because the generated artifacts made it
experientially present.

Names made parts easy to imagine. Diagrams made relationships appear settled.
Interfaces turned abstractions into things that could be clicked. Separate
agents supplied apparent independent attention. A long decision record gave
the system a history.

That feeling was real. It was evidence of imaginative and interface resonance,
not evidence of demand, benefit, defensibility, or a viable route to payment.

AI makes this failure easier because it can produce the normal signs of an
advancing product at very low cost and high speed. The evidence that usually
arrives slowly from customers can be visually and verbally simulated before
any customer has responded.

## Sequence of failure

1. A genuine practitioner observation generated an interesting hypothesis.
2. Research agents were asked to find opportunity space around it.
3. The preferred hypothesis became the primary hypothesis.
4. Product names turned tentative functions into an instrument family.
5. Architecture made the family appear internally necessary.
6. Prototypes made the proposed system tangible.
7. Positioning work made it appear ready for an audience.
8. Later agents inherited the earlier artifacts as context, which increased
   the apparent maturity of the idea.
9. Artifact volume and coherence began to substitute for external validation.

No single step was absurd. The cumulative inference was wrong.

## Language that accelerated the failure

Examples included:

- "the early signal is already quite sharp";
- "a more distinctive Oceanheart opening";
- "the central finding";
- "the sharper opening";
- "a coherent line you have been missing";
- "why this can survive better general assistants".

These phrases converted a research hypothesis into a directional conclusion
before customer, behavioural, or payment evidence existed.

The status word "Accepted" also carried two meanings. In the design log it
meant that Kai endorsed or authorized an idea. In ordinary product language it
can sound as though users or the market accepted it.

## Prevention contract

The following guard is proposed for later governance review. It is recorded
here but is not yet installed in `AGENTS.md` or automation.

Before an agent expands a product idea, it should record:

- the current external evidence;
- the decision the next artifact will change;
- the cheapest disconfirming test;
- the time budget;
- the kill condition;
- the opportunity being displaced.

Product elaboration should stop when the next artifact adds internal detail but
no external evidence.

The following work should require an explicit promotion decision:

- an umbrella name or product family;
- more than one interface variant;
- shared architecture or persistent storage;
- a product or marketing page;
- scheduled or standing automation;
- a second product hypothesis running in parallel.

Every multi-agent opportunity review should include an equally empowered
disconfirmation pass that asks what to cut, what existing product already
solves the problem, and what evidence would make the whole proposal unworthy of
further time.

## Evidence ladder

Future opportunity work should keep these states separate:

1. Operator resonance. The idea feels meaningful to its originator.
2. Repeated problem evidence. Independent people report the problem before
   hearing the proposed solution.
3. Behavioural evidence. A bounded test changes performance or decisions.
4. Commitment evidence. Someone contributes time, access, a referral, or a
   defined pilot.
5. Payment evidence. A buyer pays or enters a real procurement path.
6. Retention or outcome evidence. Use continues or produces the intended
   result.

An artifact may help move between states. Its existence is not a state change.

## What remains useful

The case preserves several useful ideas and controls. Their interest does not
reopen the product programme.

- source and inference should remain visibly distinct;
- live cues should be judged by benefit per interruption;
- model influence on professional judgment must remain inspectable;
- technical feasibility is separate from utility, distribution, and payment;
- synthetic work can answer interaction questions without authorizing live use.

The primary value of the morning is now this failure record and the prevention
method it supports.
