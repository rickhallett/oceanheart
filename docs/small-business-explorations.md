# Small-business page explorations

Public review index: https://www.oceanheart.ai/small-business

Fifteen independent landing-page directions live under that route. The main navigation is unchanged. All new routes carry `noindex, nofollow`; they are public review pages, not access-controlled pages. The existing React/vinext static export and Vercel Git deployment own delivery.

The service is practical AI guidance: identify an appropriate task, work through an example together, teach checking and independent reuse, and agree additional help separately. The copy avoids invented testimonials, guaranteed savings and unapproved price points. The free introductory conversation uses the existing digital-consultation booking link. Paid sessions are scoped separately. Rick is the established public name on the site.

The gallery offers audience filters, individual URLs, previous/next controls and a browser-local shortlist. It sends no shortlist data to a server. Review controls can be hidden on individual desktop pages.

## Design

Uses existing Oceanheart near-black #070a0d, ivory #eee9df and sea-grey #a1b7ba / #637c83. Restrained Arial/Helvetica typography and tracked wordmark are extended with occasional editorial Georgia. Layouts include a high-street panorama, cinematic evening, quote worktable, conversation, café menu, enquiry flow, makers editorial, guided steps, questions, evening poster, office documents, team session, personal letter, quote journey and compact offer.

## Original illustrations

Built-in ImageGen produced the following assets, saved as WebP under `website/public/images/small-business/`. Original PNGs remain in the generating session's local generated_images directory. The existing public Rick portrait is reused on the introduction page. Example quote and workflow diagrams are HTML and explicitly illustrative.

- `high-street.webp`: panoramic British independent high street with café, florist, repair shop and salon. Contemporary sophisticated screenprint and cut-paper editorial illustration, 3:2, balanced architectural shapes and small human figures. Ivory and sea-grey on near-black, tiny amber windows, tactile ink and paper grain. No legible text, logos, watermark, neon or robots.
- `worktable.webp`: intimate overhead view of two people working side by side, hands, laptop, notebook, mug and invoices. Editorial charcoal and coloured pencil on ivory, 3:2, generous negative space, sea-grey and near-black. No legible text, logos, watermark, neon or robots.
- `closing.webp`: a closed notebook, pencil, keys, cup, receipt and flower stem in evening window light. Refined woodcut and risograph, 3:2, quiet still life in near-black, ivory, sea-grey and subtle amber, tactile ink and carved lines. No text, logos, watermark, neon or robots.
