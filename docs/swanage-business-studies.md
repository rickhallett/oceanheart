# Swanage business studies

Three independent, non-commissioned demo studies at `/small-business/swanage`, with routes for `carve`, `tied` and `salt-pig`. They extend the existing demo gallery and retain noindex metadata. None of the businesses are represented as clients or endorsers. No business is contacted and no content is submitted to their services. Review/enquiry samples are fictional and explicitly labelled; no ratings or customer identities are used.

Each study includes source imagery, original generated artwork, nine prepared examples in total, and practical teaching outcomes. Example switches display authored samples, not live AI output. Existing consultation links are reused. Icons remain inline SVG.
