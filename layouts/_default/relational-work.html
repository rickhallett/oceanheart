The shape of it

A real situation, held by a real person.

This is a bounded piece of work around a situation that needs attention. It may begin with a conversation, a silence, a question, a boundary, or a closer look at the environment around the people involved.

I do not decide the form before meeting the situation. I do make the frame explicit before we begin.

What we are looking at, and what sits outside the work.
Basis
The professional basis on which I am offering the work.
