# oceanheart.ai

> Richard Hallett builds dependable AI automations and customer systems. He works directly with founders and internal teams to turn repeated operational work into software people can run, inspect, and recover when something fails.

This file is for agents and LLMs. Every link below is canonical; page content is plain HTML with no client-side rendering, so fetch freely. A prospective client should start at {{ "work-with-me/" | absURL }}.

Richard brings more than six years of professional software delivery and fifteen years as a cognitive behavioural therapist. The combination is relevant to process discovery, high-trust customer work, technical delivery, and clear handoff to non-technical owners.

He has a particular interest in wellbeing businesses whose founder is still the manual connection between enquiries, payments, content, client communication, and delivery. He automates administrative and product workflows, not clinical judgement.
